# How Voice treats what you tell it

**Who you are.** Voice does not ask your name, your address or anything else that identifies you.
You are a pseudonymous participant. Keep your recovery credential somewhere safe: it is the only
way back to your conversations, and Voice cannot recover it for you.

**What stays private.** Everything you write in a conversation is private to you. It is kept
encrypted under a key that belongs to you alone. No one else reads it, and it never appears on a
public page.

**What you can share.** Nothing leaves your conversation unless you choose to share it. When you
share, you see the exact words that will be shared and exactly who will see them, and you approve
them first. Shared words are checked by a person for anything that could identify you before anyone
else sees them. You can withdraw what you shared at any time.

**How AI is used.** Voice uses an AI model to reply to you and to suggest a short summary of your
concern. The model is sent your conversation in order to do that. Its suggestions are only
suggestions: nothing it writes is recorded as yours until you confirm it, and you can edit or
discard anything it proposes. The AI does not decide what is published, what is true, or what
happens next. People do, and every action on an issue names the person who recorded it.

**Your data, your choice.** You can see what Voice holds about you and ask for it to be erased.
Erasure makes your private records unreadable everywhere and removes your interests and updates.
Words you shared without your name stay on the public issue as anonymous history, marked as from an
author who has been erased. If you do not return for a long period, Voice erases your data in the
same way, and marks what remains as from an author who did not return.
